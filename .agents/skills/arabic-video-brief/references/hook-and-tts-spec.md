# Hook & TTS Specification (V2 Pipeline)

## Opening Hook
- Preserve the current title-based hook: faithful Arabic translation of the original YouTube title, followed by the description-directed suffix `ملخص الفكرة في ثوانٍ، والتفاصيل الكاملة في صندوق الوصف`.
- Preserve an original Arabic source title, subject to the existing episode/hashtag cleanup.
- Do not create an independent replacement hook or expand narration to the summary pages or ending question.
- Keep visible video content in Modern Standard Arabic. Custom visual hook fields remain supported; the TTS script is always the translated title plus the current suffix so stale supplied hook text cannot restore the old ending.

## ElevenLabs Narration
- Use the existing ElevenLabs configuration, voice selection, and voice settings. No new paid plan or alternative robotic narrator is required.
- Configuration remains `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, `ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID`, `ELEVENLABS_MODEL_ID`, and `ELEVENLABS_LANGUAGE_CODE` (`ar`).
- Preserve natural cadence and current audio normalization; do not force the title into a 2.4-second window or accelerate it artificially.
- Keep the existing music ducking and recorded outro; the new ending question is on-screen text only.
- If the key is absent or generation fails, retain the current graceful visual-only fallback.

Previously exported videos and saved `hook-voice.mp3` files are not regenerated by this skill update. The new script applies to new V2 processing. A timing-only rerender reuses the existing recording; replacing that recording requires a new TTS synthesis run.
